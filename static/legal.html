Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ClipForge ("we", "us") provides video clipping and editing services and operates our own social media channels. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send through our website form or by email: your name, email address, channel link and message.
  • Videos and media you send us, or that you authorize us to use, so we can make clips for you.
  • Account connections. We connect our own YouTube and TikTok accounts to our software to publish videos to those accounts. We store the access tokens those services issue to us. We do not connect to or post on any client's social accounts.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address, used only to prevent spam and abuse.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the services you ask for.
  • To deliver clips through private preview links.
  • To publish our own original content to our own channels.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Data from YouTube and TikTok

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy. Our app uses the YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We use TikTok's APIs only to upload videos to our own TikTok account as drafts. You can revoke our access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to a TikTok account in TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions.

How long we keep it

Client videos and clips are kept while you are a client and deleted within 90 days after you ask or the engagement ends. Contact messages are kept for up to 2 years. Platform tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing officialclipforge@gmail.com.

Security

Our dashboard is password-protected and served over HTTPS. Preview links are private, hard-to-guess URLs; anyone with the link can view the clips on it, so share them carefully.
